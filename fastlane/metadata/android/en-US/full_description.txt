<p>Megalith LiDAR is an open-source field survey atlas of unrecorded Prehistoric, Iron Age, and Roman earthwork candidates across England, detected from 1 m Environment Agency bare-earth LiDAR and 16 m aerial cropmarks.</p>

<p>Every candidate is cross-checked against 1888–1913 Ordnance Survey Six-Inch maps, Historic England's National Heritage List for England (NHLE), OpenStreetMap archaeology and modern confounders, and Megalithic.co.uk.</p>

<p><b>Key Features:</b></p>
<ul>
  <li><b>Offline Bundled Survey Dataset:</b> Browse Prehistoric barrows, henges, Roman villas/camps/aggers, Iron Age hillforts, settlement enclosures, and rediscovered Scheduled Monuments across English counties with no account or tracking.</li>
  <li><b>Interactive Multi-Layer Map &amp; 7-Panel Site Inspector:</b> Switch between 1 m LiDAR DTM Bare-Earth Hillshade, 1 m LiDAR RVT Multi-Hillshade, 1 m LiDAR DSM Canopy, 1888–1913 OS Six-Inch maps, Roman Empire Atlas (DARE), BGS 1:50k Bedrock Geology, and aerial imagery, with geo-draped 256 m × 256 m slope, 16 m local relief, canopy, AI probability, and aerial cropmark overlays.</li>
  <li><b>One-Tap OsmAnd &amp; Field GPS Integration:</b> Export the custom OsmAnd plugin (.osf) with modular GPX track layers, Roman and ancient ridgeway routes, and custom 1 m LiDAR / 1888 OS map sources, or export filtered GPX waypoints directly to OsmAnd, Organic Maps, Garmin, or QGIS.</li>
  <li><b>Near Me GPS &amp; Field Survey Log:</b> Sort candidates by distance from your live GPS position and record ground-truthing verdicts, Historic Environment Record (HER) references, and field observations locally with JSON import/export.</li>
</ul>
