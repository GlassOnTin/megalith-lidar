Initial F-Droid release (v3.2.0):
- Native Android app wrapping the UK 1 m LiDAR & Aerial Cropmark Monument Survey atlas with bundled Leaflet 1.9.4 and offline England candidate dataset.
- Interactive 7-panel 256 m site inspector (bare-earth slope, 16 m local relief, surface canopy, model probability, 16 m aerial cropmarks, and 1888 OS Six-Inch map cross-checks).
- Native Android FileProvider sharing for OsmAnd plugin (.osf), filtered GPX waypoints (.gpx), and field survey JSON notes.
