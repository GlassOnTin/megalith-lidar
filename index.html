UK 1 m LiDAR & Aerial Cropmark Monument Survey

Multi-domain scale-space terrain & 16 m aerial cropmark screening for unrecorded Prehistoric, Iron Age, and Roman earthworks across England

Quick-Start Guide & Survey Scope: Out-of-sample 2 km × 2 km square survey (24,424 km² across 6,106 squares) — click any table row or map pin to inspect its 7-panel 1 m LiDAR, 16 m Cropmark & 1888 OS imagery, or export to OsmAnd. How to use ▾
1. Choose a Domain Tab or Search (Left Panel) Switch between Prehistoric, Roman, and Iron Age & Settlement candidates, filter by 1888 OS Map Antiquity ('Tumulus' / 'Camp'), or search by OS Grid Ref, keyword, or Near Me.
2. Click Any Row or Map Pin (Right Panel) Selecting a site updates the shareable permalink (#site=...) and displays its 7-panel 1 m LiDAR, 16 m Aerial Cropmark & 1888 OS 6-Inch Map breakdown, walkability badge, and academic assessment.
3. Field Walking with OsmAnd / GPS Tap OsmAnd Plugin (.osf) on Android/iOS to install all monument Favourites, Roman & Ancient Road tracks, and the 1888 OS 6-Inch map overlay into OsmAnd in one tap, or use Export Visible (.gpx) for any filtered subset.
