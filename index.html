UK 1 m LiDAR & Aerial Cropmark Monument Survey

Multi-domain scale-space terrain & 16 m aerial cropmark screening for unrecorded Prehistoric, Iron Age, and Roman earthworks across England
Inspired by Adam Morgan Ibbotson's Thornborough LiDAR video · GitHub Repository (AGPL-3.0) · Download GeoJSON · Raw Summary JSON
1. Start with "Unidentified Candidates" (Left Panel) The default tab lists terrain anomalies that match prehistoric earthwork geometry in 1 m LiDAR and are not listed in Historic England's Scheduled Monument register or mapped as modern features in OpenStreetMap.
2. Click Any Row or Map Pin to Inspect (Right Panel) Selecting a site zooms the map to its British National Grid coordinates and displays its 6-panel 1 m LiDAR & 16 m Aerial Cropmark/Soilmark breakdown on the right, with a direct link to inspect it on OpenStreetMap.
3. Check the National Coverage Map (Right Panel) Important: This is a statistically sampled out-of-sample survey, not a full wall-to-wall scan of Britain. The national map shows the exact 2 km × 2 km sample squares (5,224.0 km² across 1,306 squares, ~4.01% of England) scanned across all three detectors.
