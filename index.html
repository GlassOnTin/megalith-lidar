UK 1 m LiDAR Prehistoric Monument Survey

Automated terrain screening for unrecorded barrows, henges, and megalithic earthworks in Environment Agency 1 m LiDAR
Inspired by Adam Morgan Ibbotson's LiDAR survey video · GitHub Repository (AGPL-3.0) · Download GeoJSON · Raw Summary JSON
1. Start with "Unidentified Candidates" (Left Panel) The default tab lists terrain anomalies that match prehistoric earthwork geometry in 1 m LiDAR and are not listed in Historic England's Scheduled Monument register or mapped as modern features in OpenStreetMap.
2. Click Any Row or Map Pin to Inspect (Right Panel) Selecting a site zooms the map to its British National Grid coordinates and displays its 4-panel 1 m LiDAR elevation breakdown on the right, with a direct link to inspect it on OpenStreetMap.
3. Switch Tabs for Known Sites or Model Statistics Use the tabs above the list if you want to see already-known Scheduled Monuments rediscovered during blind testing, modern quarries/sinkholes filtered out via OpenStreetMap, or ROC-AUC statistical benchmarks.
